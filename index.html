About Me

I've been capturing moments through photography since 2015, specializing in weddings, portraits, lifestyle, events, and travel photography.

Originally from Nepal — surrounded by the beauty of the Himalayas, mountains, and adventure-filled landscapes — my passion for photography began through travel and storytelling. Inspired by nature, culture, and genuine human connection, I developed a creative eye for capturing authentic moments and emotions.

Self-taught and driven by passion, I refined my craft through years of hands-on experience and artistic exploration. My style is mostly candid and cinematic — focused on real emotions, natural interactions, beautiful light, and timeless storytelling. Rather than overly posed images, I aim to preserve moments as they naturally unfold.

Whether documenting intimate weddings, meaningful portraits, or unforgettable adventures, every shoot comes back to one purpose: capturing moments that matter.
